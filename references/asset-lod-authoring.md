# Asset LOD authoring

## Problem and design reasoning

A simplified asset can satisfy a triangle target while losing its defining silhouette, changing material response, or revealing a seam at a modular boundary. Authoring therefore needs a visible error budget alongside a cost budget. Guerrilla's *Killzone: Shadow Fall* demo postmortem reports several character detail levels with lower polygon counts and reduced bone influences as detail decreases. Its data illustrates that geometry and deformation costs both belong in the authored representation. These are historical production figures, and target values should come from the current project. [Guerrilla, Killzone Shadow Fall Demo Postmortem](https://www.guerrilla-games.com/read/killzone-shadow-fall-demo-postmortem) · [Guerrilla presentation, character LOD data](https://www.guerrilla-games.com/media/News/Files/Valient_Killzone_Shadow_Fall_Demo_Postmortem.pdf)

Naughty Dog's GDC presentation describes rebuilding meshes and transferring high-resolution detail while dealing with UV-border seams in *Uncharted 2*. The authoring workflow below is a portable recommendation. It applies those fidelity concerns to a chain of lower-cost representations and requires testing the chain in its intended camera conditions. [Richard Diamant and Judd Simantov, Uncharted 2 Character Pipeline, GDC 2010](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1012798/Uncharted-2-Character-Pipeline-An)

## Techniques and practical checks

- **Choose features to preserve before simplification.** Mark the outline, material boundaries, contact points, deformation regions, and modular edges that matter at the intended view size. Assign simplification tolerances by those features rather than applying the same reduction ratio to every asset. **Check** compare front, side, and oblique views of adjacent LODs at their intended pixel coverage. Record lost silhouette features and boundary movement against the project's acceptance tolerance.
- **Validate the complete exported asset.** Review normals, tangent splits, UV padding, material slots, bone influences, and socket requirements after generating lower detail. Re-bake maps when simplification changes the tangent-space relationship enough to create visible errors. Keep collision representation under an explicit gameplay policy. **Check** inspect each LOD under reference lighting and the shipping material. Run extreme supported poses and place modular neighbors beside it. Verify seam continuity, stable contacts, and readable material edges.
- **Author a reusable comparison scene.** Use camera rails and scale markers to reveal the actual transition view. Include the reference asset and its simplified chain in the same build, with the ability to freeze a representation for comparison. Validate changes under supported fields of view, resolutions, and platform quality settings. **Check** move slowly through every switch point, then test fast traversal and a camera cut. Compare stills and video for popping, shading shifts, and deformation changes.
- **Keep generated variants reproducible.** Track source revision, simplifier and bake settings, and any manual corrections. Validate the exported chain automatically for missing levels, unexpected material slots, excessive influences, and invalid bounds. Reassess total cost when a lower-poly version increases materials or opacity coverage. **Check** regenerate one asset from a clean input revision, compare its reported budgets, and measure a representative population in-game. Confirm a source change invalidates the affected variants and preserves intentional artist corrections.
